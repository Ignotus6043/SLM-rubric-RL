## RaR-Science-Static

RaR-Science-Static is derived from
[`anisha2102/RaR-Science`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/anisha2102/RaR-Science).
The released static bank also contains responses generated by
[`Qwen/Qwen3-4B`](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B) and criterion labels
produced with `gpt-4o-2024-11-20`. The upstream RaR-Science dataset card did
not state a license when this repository was prepared. Users must follow the
current terms of RaR-Science, Qwen3, and the relevant model/API providers.
